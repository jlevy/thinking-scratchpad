== what this program cannot decide ==
1. It cannot decide any composition at a side at or above B (2 + (4/3) sqrt 2) = 3.876681 for
   a class containing both ends of the arc, whatever the site set. The shrink costs 0.008937
   of side and Stromquist's headroom above Trump's value is 0.008534.
2. It cannot recover Stromquist's Theorem 3. That theorem adds a box step -- twelve marked
   points, three of them forced into one box -- which is a conditional certificate (X-014
   Lemma 2) and needs a non-convex admissible centre domain. This program has no admissible
   domain hook at all; sweep.centre_domain, the float mirror in generate.py and the four
   half-planes interval.DirectionSearch propagates are untouched here by design.
3. It cannot certify anything. It refutes one composition of one net at one side. It is not a
   five-condition Certificate, devtools.decide_certificate does not decide it, and no class
   certificate is retained by this cell.
4. It cannot separate a site-set artefact from a covering wall on one site set. The 16.000000
   at grid 23 and at inset 1/5 is the known round-number signature; five site sets were run
   at U for that reason.
5. It cannot decide that a composition survives. The program is one-sided: a converged
   optimum below n0 w0 + n1 w1 on any site set refutes, because restricting the sites can
   only raise the optimum, but an optimum at or above it says only that this site set
   failed. The 19-cell class at Trump's side reads 9.543210 on one site set and 11.000000
   on two others, and the refutation is the sound reading of the three.
6. It says nothing about the compositions between the two controls. Only (11, 0) was decided,
   on two classes and eight near-axis widths, plus (9, 2) priced but not decided.
